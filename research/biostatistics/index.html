04 · PlanAnalysis FrameworkOutcomes · covariates · reliability · missing data · small-N interpretationView framework

Outcomes

Satisfaction, mental-health understanding, service experience, and placement-stability measures.

Covariates / stratifiers

Respondent group, evaluation context, timepoint, service exposure, and prespecified contextual variables.

Descriptive summaries

Counts, valid denominators, distributions, medians or proportions, and item-level missingness.

Reliability / consistency

Item behavior and internal consistency only when scale structure and sample size make the estimate appropriate.

Missing data

Review patterns and reasons, report analysis-specific denominators, and avoid unexamined imputation.

Small-N interpretation

Emphasize uncertainty and effect size, suppress unstable cells, and avoid overinterpreting subgroup estimates.

Planned components only. No results, effects, participant records, or clinical outcomes are claimed.
