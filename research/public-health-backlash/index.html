Public Health Backlash Lab

Built and prepared the source-data layer for a multi-source quantitative study of public-health backlash, cleaning and harmonizing CDC, OxCGRT, and election datasets across state, county, and daily structures; assessed dataset coverage, standardized variables and formats, and conducted qualitative coding and analytic memoing on policy response and public trust.

05 · ReviewDataset AssessmentCoverage · granularity · identifiers · integration considerationsRead assessment

CDC · integration read

  • County-by-day structure with state and county FIPS identifiers.
  • Coverage: Apr 2020–Aug 2021.
  • Policy flag and order code are usable; descriptive fields are sparse in places.
  • State-month or county-month aggregation is needed for lower-resolution joins.

OxCGRT · integration read

  • Daily national and subnational policy series with standardized region codes.
  • Wide raw schema includes indicators, flags, notes, indexes, and epidemiological fields.
  • Jurisdiction variants require deliberate filtering.
  • Index and indicator selection must be documented.

MIT Election Lab · integration read

  • Candidate-level returns require aggregation for political-context measures.
  • State FIPS and postal identifiers support linkage.
  • Election observations are periodic, not daily; carry-forward rules must be explicit.
  • Relevant-year filters remove unrelated historical records.
